TIPS Academy · Enrollment Confirmed

You’re Enrolled. Welcome to the Academy.

Your payment has secured your seat. The founding cohorts begin on Monday, January 11, 2027. Here is exactly what happens next — save this page.

Your next steps, in order

  1. Your receipt. Stripe emails your payment receipt immediately. Keep it — it is your proof of enrollment.
  2. Your welcome email. Within one business day, the Academy admissions office writes to the email you paid with, confirming your program, your cohort and its start date.
  3. Your training access. Your course opens on the TIPS Campus at your cohort’s start; your welcome email carries your sign-in details and your first week’s schedule.
  4. Train, then be assessed. Complete your modules, sit the knowledge examination (pass mark 70%), then present your practical assessment (pass mark 70%).
  5. Your certificate. Pass both, and TIPS issues your certificate — your name enters The Roll, and anyone can verify it on the Verify page.

If you enrolled in CES — your four weeks

  • Weeks 1–3: Foundations of Public Speaking · Overcoming Stage Fright · Speech Structure · Storytelling & Message Development · Voice, Body Language & Stage Presence · Audience Engagement · Practical Speech Delivery.
  • Week 4: the knowledge examination, then you submit your recorded speech for the practical assessment.
  • On passing both: Certified Emerging Speaker (CES) — and the door to CPS is open whenever you are ready.

If you enrolled in CPS, CPTC or the Complete Pathway

  • CPS (8–12 weeks): keynote craft, positioning and the speaking business, closing with the knowledge examination and your assessed professional keynote.
  • CPTC (12–16 weeks): training design, facilitation and coaching, closing with the examination and your assessed training or coaching demonstration.
  • Complete Pathway: CES opens first; each next level unlocks the moment you pass the one before it — no further tuition, ever.

Questions before your welcome email arrives? Write to the admissions office at ceo@tolkzspeakers.com with your Stripe receipt attached, and we will answer within one business day.
