TIPS Academy · Complete Pathway

One Enrollment. Three Certifications.

The Complete Certification Pathway enrolls you in all three Academy programs at once — Certified Emerging Speaker, Certified Professional Speaker, and Certified Professional Trainer & Coach — at one pathway price, unlocked in order as you earn each level.

How the pathway unlocks

  • 1. CES unlocks immediately. Begin the Certified Emerging Speaker program (4 weeks) as soon as your enrollment is confirmed.
  • 2. CPS unlocks when you pass CES. Complete CES training and pass its assessments, and the Certified Professional Speaker program (8–12 weeks) opens to you — no second payment.
  • 3. CPTC unlocks when you pass CPS. Pass CPS, and the Certified Professional Trainer & Coach program (12–16 weeks) opens — again, no further tuition.

One certificate per completed level — earned, never bundled. Buying the pathway enrolls you in all three programs, but it earns you nothing by itself. Each level's certificate is issued only when that level's training is complete and its examinations and practical assessments are passed.

One pathway payment covers all three programs — you are never charged again as each level unlocks, and never charged automatically for anything else.

Leave and resume

Life does not always move in a straight line. Pathway students may leave and resume their studies in line with the Academy's program-access policy. Every level you have completed — and every certificate you have earned — stays permanently on your student record, and you return to exactly the level where you stopped.

Prefer one step at a time?

The pathway is a choice, never a requirement. You can enroll in CES, CPS, or CPTC on its own — including by approved direct entry at the CPS and CPTC levels — and pay only for the program you take.
