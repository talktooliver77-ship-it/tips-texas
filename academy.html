From training to certificate — the path at every level

The route is identical at all three levels, so you always know exactly where you stand:

  1. Enroll & train. Apply below, confirm your tuition for your country of residence, and complete your program's training modules.
  2. Knowledge examination. Sit the level's examination and score at least 70%.
  3. Practical assessment. Present your assessed work — a speech at CES, a professional keynote at CPS, a training or coaching demonstration at CPTC — and score at least 70%.
  4. Certificate issued. Pass both, and TIPS issues your certificate: your name enters The Roll of certified speakers, and anyone can confirm it on the Verify page.
  5. Stay certified. Renew every three years (below) and your standing continues without interruption.

No certificate is issued on attendance or experience alone — at every level, both assessments must be passed. That is what makes a TIPS certificate worth holding.

Admissions — enrollment is open

You can begin today. The founding cohorts are forming now, in Cameroon and internationally:

  1. Apply. Complete the short application form — it takes about three minutes.
  2. Confirm. We confirm your program, your entry route and your exact tuition for your country of residence by email.
  3. Pay. Pay in full, or secure your seat with a deposit where installment plans apply — Cameroon residents pay by MTN Mobile Money; all other residents receive a secure card payment link with their confirmation.
  4. Train. Your program opens, and your path to the examination is set out from day one.

Four journeys are served, each with its own route on the one form:

  • Student A — CES only: a beginner enrolls directly in the Certified Emerging Speaker program. Open enrollment, no experience required.
  • Student B — direct entry to CPS: an experienced speaker applies for direct entry to the Certified Professional Speaker program, with evidence of practice. Admission by approval — the examinations and assessments still apply in full.
  • Student C — direct entry to CPTC: an experienced trainer or coach applies for advanced direct entry to the Certified Professional Trainer & Coach program, with evidence of training or coaching practice.
  • Student D — the Complete Pathway: one enrollment for all three certifications, unlocked level by level as each is passed.

No student is ever charged automatically for another certification.
