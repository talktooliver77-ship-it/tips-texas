Founding cohort in session — the Academy's first full intake opens in 2027. The three certification programs below are the Academy's permanent structure: explore them now, and be ready when admissions open.

Beginner Level

Certified Emerging Speaker (CES)

For beginners, students, aspiring public speakers, young professionals, entrepreneurs, and anyone seeking real communication confidence.

  • Duration: 4 weeks
  • Foundations of public speaking & confidence
  • Speech structure, storytelling & message craft
  • Voice, body language & stage presence
  • Assessed final speech
  • Tuition: 25,000 FCFA (Cameroon residents) · $99 (International)
  • Entry: Open enrollment — no experience required

Professional Level

Certified Professional Speaker (CPS)

For practicing speakers, aspiring keynote speakers, motivational speakers, corporate presenters, entrepreneurs, and communication professionals.

  • Duration: 8–12 weeks
  • Professional speaker identity & positioning
  • Advanced speechwriting & keynote craft
  • Audience psychology & cross-cultural communication
  • Speaking business models, pricing & client acquisition
  • Assessed professional keynote presentation
  • Tuition: 75,000 FCFA (Cameroon residents) · $299 (International)
  • Entry: CES completion, or approved experience-based direct entry
View Program → Apply Now →
Advanced Level

Certified Professional Trainer & Coach (CPTC)

For experienced speakers, trainers, teachers, facilitators, coaches, consultants, HR professionals, and corporate training specialists.

  • Duration: 12–16 weeks
  • Adult learning & instructional design
  • Workshop facilitation & group dynamics
  • Coaching techniques & client development
  • Training evaluation & performance measurement
  • Assessed training or coaching demonstration
  • Tuition: 150,000 FCFA (Cameroon residents) · $599 (International)
  • Entry: CPS completion, or approved advanced direct entry

Tuition is determined by your country of residence, not your nationality. Cameroon residents pay in FCFA; international students pay in USD.

Best Value

The Complete Certification Pathway

One enrollment covering all three certifications — CES, CPS, and CPTC — unlocked in order as you pass each level. Bought separately, the three programs cost 250,000 FCFA (Cameroon) or $997 (International). The Complete Pathway is 225,000 FCFA or $899 — a saving of 25,000 FCFA or $98.

  • CES unlocks immediately; CPS unlocks after CES; CPTC unlocks after CPS
  • One certificate per completed level — each one earned by examination and assessment
  • Pause and resume under the Academy's program-access policy

Admissions — opening with the 2027 intake

Admissions placeholder: the Academy's enrollment and application forms will open on this page with the 2027 intake. Four journeys will be served, each with its own form:

  • Student A — CES only: a beginner enrolls directly in the Certified Emerging Speaker program. Open enrollment, no experience required.
  • Student B — direct entry to CPS: an experienced speaker applies for direct entry to the Certified Professional Speaker program, with evidence of practice. Admission by approval — the examinations and assessments still apply in full.
  • Student C — direct entry to CPTC: an experienced trainer or coach applies for advanced direct entry to the Certified Professional Trainer & Coach program, with evidence of training or coaching practice.
  • Student D — the Complete Pathway: one enrollment for all three certifications, unlocked level by level as each is passed.

Every form will ask for your country of residence, because tuition follows residence — Cameroon residents pay in FCFA, international students in USD. No student is ever charged automatically for another certification.

Institutional partnerships

The Texas Institute of Public Speaking (TIPS) owns and operates the Academy: it delivers the training, administers the examinations and assessments, keeps student records, and issues every Academy certificate. TIPS is in discussion with the Cameroon National Speakers Association (CANASA) regarding a proposed professional certification partnership. Any such arrangement will be announced on this page if and when a formal agreement is concluded.
