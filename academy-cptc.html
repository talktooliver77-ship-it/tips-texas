TIPS Academy · Advanced Level

Certified Professional Trainer & Coach (CPTC)

The Academy's highest certification — for experienced speakers, trainers, teachers, facilitators, coaches, consultants, HR professionals, and corporate training specialists.

Program at a glance

  • Level: Advanced
  • Duration: 12–16 weeks
  • Entry: CPS completion, or an approved advanced experience and competency assessment
  • Tuition by residence: 150,000 FCFA (Cameroon) · $599 (International) · $2,995 (United States) · $2,995 CAD (Canada) · €2,995 (Europe)

Tuition is set by your country of residence, not your nationality. Cameroon residents pay in FCFA. Residents of the United States, Canada and Europe pay the same numerals in their own currency — USD, CAD and EUR. Students residing anywhere else pay the international USD rate.

What you will study

  1. Foundations of Professional Training and Coaching
  2. Adult Learning Principles
  3. Curriculum and Instructional Design
  4. Workshop Facilitation and Group Dynamics
  5. Coaching Techniques and Client Development
  6. Training Evaluation and Performance Measurement
  7. Professional Training Business Development
  8. Corporate Training Proposals and Contracts
  9. Professional Ethics and Coaching Boundaries
  10. Final Training Demonstration and Competency Assessment

What certification requires

  • Complete all required modules.
  • Pass the examination with at least 70%.
  • Submit a professional training plan.
  • Deliver a practical training or coaching demonstration.
  • Achieve at least 70% on the practical assessment.

Award: Certified Professional Trainer & Coach (CPTC), issued by TIPS.

Two ways in

Progression: complete the Certified Professional Speaker (CPS) program and continue directly into CPTC, paying only the CPTC tuition.

Direct entry: experienced trainers and coaches may apply for advanced direct entry, supported by an advanced experience and competency assessment. If the application is approved, you enter CPTC without first completing CPS. Applicants who do not yet meet the advanced standard are offered the appropriate preparatory training instead.

No certification is granted on the basis of experience alone. Direct entry admits you to the program — every CPTC candidate, however experienced, must still complete the required modules, submit the professional training plan, pass the examination, and pass the practical demonstration assessment.

After certification — staying certified

Your CPTC certificate is valid for three years. To renew: complete 15 continuing-education credits in the cycle (TIPS refresher trainings, Tolkz masterminds and approved speaking engagements count) and pay the renewal fee for your residence — 15,000 FCFA (Cameroon) · 295 (United States, Canada & Europe, in local currency) · $79 (other international). Your name stays on The Roll and your certificate stays verifiable throughout. CPTC holders who train others under the TIPS name also hold the annual Trainer Licence — 25,000 FCFA (Cameroon) · $99 (International) · 495 (United States, Canada & Europe, in local currency).

The top of the pathway

CPTC completes the Academy pathway: CES → CPS → CPTC. Each certificate you have earned along the way remains permanently on your record, whether you arrived level by level or by approved direct entry.
