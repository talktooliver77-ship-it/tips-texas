TIPS Academy · Admissions

Apply & Enroll

Enrollment is open. Complete this short application and we will confirm your program, your entry route and your exact tuition for your country of residence — then your training begins.

How enrollment works

  1. Apply — this form takes about three minutes.
  2. Confirm — you receive an email confirming your program and tuition.
  3. Pay — Cameroon residents pay by MTN Mobile Money; all other residents receive a secure card payment link with their confirmation. Deposit-and-installment plans apply on CPS, CPTC and the Complete Pathway.
  4. Train — your program opens, with your examination path set out from day one: training, knowledge examination (70%+), practical assessment (70%+), certificate.

United States residents — enroll instantly

If you are a U.S. resident entering CES (open enrollment), progressing from a completed TIPS level, or you already hold an approved direct-entry decision, you may skip the form and pay securely now:

CES — Pay $495 →   CPS — Pay $1,495 →   Complete Pathway — Pay $3,995 →   CPTC — Pay $2,995 →

Everyone else — and every direct-entry applicant — the form below is your route.

Submitting opens your email app with the application addressed to the TIPS Academy admissions office (ceo@tolkzspeakers.com). If nothing opens, write to us directly at that address.
